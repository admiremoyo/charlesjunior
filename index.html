Who we are

A builder you can call, and a team you will actually see on site

Charles & Juniour Projects (Private) Limited is led by its directors — Mr C. Charles, R. Juniour and C. Maplan — who stay personally involved in every job we take on. From a single cottage to a multi-level development, the same standard applies: proper drawings, honest quotations, quality materials and workmanship we are happy to put our name to.

  • Design and construction under one roof — plans, approvals and building by one team
  • Qualified crews for each trade: steel fixing, roofing, electrics, plumbing and finishes
  • Transparent, itemised quotations before any work begins
  • Materials sourced and delivered through our own hardware supply arm
Plans & approvals

Your project starts on our drawing board

Before a single brick is laid, our draughting team prepares full architectural and structural drawings — floor plans, elevations and structural details ready for council submission. We also carry out site setting-out and levelling, so what is on paper is exactly what gets built.

What we do

Services

Building Construction

Full builds from siteworks, foundations and ground beams through brickwork, ring beams and slabs — residential and commercial.

Roofing

Timber trusses, sheeting and concrete tile roofing, fitted with proper covering, flashing and waterproofing details.

Steel Fixing & Structures

Reinforcement cutting, bending and fixing for footings, columns, beams, slabs and retaining structures — done to the engineer's detail.

Carpentry & Fitted Kitchens

Custom kitchens, built-in cupboards and joinery, finished with quality boards, stone tops and soft-close fittings.

Ceilings, Skimming & Painting

Plastering and skimming, bulkhead and suspended ceilings with LED detail, and interior or exterior painting.

Driveways & Paving

Interlocking pavers, kerbing and surfacing for driveways, walkways and yards — plus tennis court construction.
