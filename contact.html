Common questions

Before you ask

Do you only work in Harare?

No — we are based in Belvedere, Harare, but we take on projects across Zimbabwe. For jobs outside Harare we will talk through site logistics with you when we quote.

Can you build from plans I already have?

Yes. Bring your approved plans and we will quote on the construction. If you do not have plans yet, our draughting team can prepare architectural and structural drawings for council submission.

Can I hire you for just one part of the job?

Certainly. Plenty of our work is single-trade: a roof, a fitted kitchen, ceilings and skimming, tiling, a driveway or a tennis court. You do not need a full build to use us.

Do you supply materials?

Yes — we have a hardware supply arm and can deliver river sand, pit sand, stone and other materials to your stand, whether or not we are doing the building.

How do I get a quotation?

Call or WhatsApp us on +263 78 455 1234, email your plans to charlesjuniorprojects@gmail.com, or use the form above. We will assess the job and give you a written, itemised quotation.
